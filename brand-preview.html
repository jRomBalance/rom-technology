Usage Guide

✅ Portfolio Website

Use the horizontal lockup in the nav. Dark background logo in the hero. Light variant for any white sections.

✅ GitHub Profile

Use the square dark logo as your profile picture. Horizontal lockup in your README header.

✅ LinkedIn

Square dark logo as profile photo. Horizontal lockup as banner image (stretch to fill).

✅ Email Signature

Horizontal lockup at 200px wide. Forest Green for your name, Slate for contact details.

✅ Heads Up Sport

The green palette is shared — your personal brand and the app brand are visually unified.

✅ Business Card

Dark background with square logo on front. Horizontal lockup + tagline on back.

❌ Don't stretch

Never distort the chip proportions. Scale uniformly only.

❌ Don't recolor

Keep the green accent consistent. Don't substitute other colors for the chip text or pins.
